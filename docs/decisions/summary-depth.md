# Summary depth — user decision (2026-10-27, revised 2026-11-16)

**Revised 2026-11-16** (user decisions). On the first NJU lecture the user found that a lecture is not one line with everything hung on it: a demonstration (data taken out of a locked-down machine through Excel formulas drawing QR codes) led to an idea of its own (have the model design an intermediate language that translates both to Python and to Excel formulas, check each translation on its own, and the whole comes out right in one go; at 01:36 the speaker adds that no model thinks of designing such a language first), and the summary kept the demonstration but lost the idea, hung one proposition of it under the main conclusion and put it in a part twenty minutes later. The rules now ask for: 要点 that leave nothing important out (every conclusion and idea the video develops, demonstration the video spends time on with what it shows, and what the speaker stresses), in the video's order and unclassified, checked part by part; 主线与旁路, grouped by theme and in logical order within a theme, indented, each side path under the point it branches off from (the general idea it leads to, where it rejoins) — corrected the same day (user: 「要点是按照时间顺序的，而主线+旁路是按照不同主题分类后内部按照逻辑顺序的（也就是需要缩进的）」), after the first version listed side paths among the 要点 and kept them in a flat list apart from the main line; additions beyond the video where it is brief (background, mechanism, the principle an example stands for, a connection to a known idea), each `补充：`, and a side path of the summarizer's own marked 补充. Also (same day): requiring every stretch of speech made summaries long-winded and overfitted, so length follows what a point is worth, stretches with nothing in them are left out without accounting for them, and the rounds name a long uncovered stretch once only (summary_review.py). The rules still name no subject or kind of video; the lecture is described here, not in them.

**Revised again 2026-11-16** (user decisions, after a rerun of the lecture with the same model still hung the Excel demonstration as support under a conclusion it does not support and kept the intermediate-language idea in the main line). The user changed the procedure: first find the self-contained structures, then see whether a main line can be merged from them, as a forest or independent graphs (「首先找出自包含的结构，然后看自包含的结构中是否可以合并出一条主线，采取森林/独立图的结构」). Of two ways to cut the units Claude offered (by support relations alone, which would fold the Excel thread into the main line through the link at 01:36, or by what the speaker develops as a block), the user took the second; a unit's root may be an event (a demonstration, a story, an observation) as well as a conclusion (「不一定非要是命题，可以是事件」). So 主线与旁路 became 主线与单元: units side by side, not nested; a main line only where several units' conclusions support a higher one, naming the units by number; links between them as `→ 支撑 **P36**` with the time the video makes them; a proposition used in several places written once (Claude judgment on these details, which the user accepted). The additions became `拓展：`, after two of three additions in the rerun retold the speaker (user: 「补充应该改成内容拓展，并把其到底是什么（不是xxx，而是xxxx）说出来」): the rules say what a 拓展 is in that form, not what the video says put elsewhere and not general knowledge, but what a reader needs and the video does not give. Claude first took the request as asking every 拓展 to say what its subject really is as 不是……而是……; the user corrected it the same day (「对 `拓展`到底是什么，用不是...而是...写在提示词里」); checking a source or the strength of support, which the rules had also put under 补充, is `核对：` (Claude judgment). No program check of additions for now (user: 「先不用检查规则」). The same day, after a rerun with that template (Antigravity, Gemini 3.8 Flash, which read no earlier version) linked every unit to the main line and kept in each unit mostly what it gave the main line, losing the Excel thread's own idea (verify each translation into the intermediate language) and putting the speaker's link at 01:36:50 into another unit, the user set the baseline: a unit has two identities, the self-contained part it develops on its own and the part that serves the main line (「这些单元应该有两个身份，一个是自包含、独立展开单元的部分，另一个是接到主线、服务主线的部分」), and the first is a tree of its own, linked to nothing, while the second is written in the main line (「自身就是树、独立的、不连接的，而服务主线的部分要写道主线里」). Under the main conclusion go the propositions that carry each unit's contribution, with the unit they come from and whether the video says the link (`视频明说`, with its time) or the summarizer infers it (`总结者归纳`). Writing the service part in both places was left out (Claude judgment, the user did not mind): two copies drift apart, and a service part inside the unit is what crowded out its own idea. Also added (Claude's proposals from the rerun): steps the speaker takes in one go (a demonstration, what went wrong, the fix) are one unit, and an event is the root or a step of its unit, never support for a conclusion it does not show. After the rerun with that template, the link label `视频明说` was read as "the video says this proposition" (two of the times given were the unit's own, one was the speaker on a different matter), so it became `讲者接上` with the time and the words of the link (user approved). The user judged the remaining faults not a matter of the model's ability (「应该不是模型能力问题」); the run had read the whole material, the line at 01:22:30 included, and two causes were found in the rules: the propositions were numbered "in the order they first appear" through the summary while 要点 and 主线与单元 come first and name 详细内容's propositions, with nothing saying which to write first (the rerun gave P53 two different meanings), and nothing said where a link the speaker makes in a later part goes (the one at 01:36 went into the unit of its part). Now the work order is 详细内容 first, numbered as it goes, then 要点, 主线与单元, 概要 and 元信息, written out in the template's order; such a link goes into the main line (Claude's fix from the run).

The user found summaries shallow. Besides the missing screen pages, the rules asked for it: the first rule, "Map, don't retell", gave only an overview, main points and a timeline unless the user asked for more (a default taken over from upstream in 0.1.0, not a user decision), `summary-context.md` asked for a "concise" summary, and 详细内容 had one line of guidance. Now 详细内容 is the body: part by part, what a viewer would note down, with a length that follows how much the video says, shortened only on request; pictures are worked in where they add something, without a duty to mention each one. The user asked that the rules name no subject or kind of video (they apply to every video) and not require every screen page.
