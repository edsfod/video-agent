# Layout and screen pages — user decision (2026-10-27)

Lecture uploads often show the camera picture beside or inside the screen recording. On the user's 97-minute example (1280x410, camera left, screen right) whole-frame sampling missed most pages: scene detection found 7 changes, the regular samples were cut to 36 (about one per 3 minutes), and everything was scaled to 960 pixels wide, so the screen half was about 550 pixels. AutoSlides Extractor kept 9 slides: it compares whole frames, so the moving speaker hides page turns, its classifier dropped 13 of about 25 candidates as not slides because they show the room, and its auto-crop (Canny or YOLO, `--ml-autocrop-maybe`) only runs afterwards on `may_be_slide` frames. The user decided that frame counts follow the video with only a runaway limit, and that layout detection and page detection run on every `process`; AutoSlides Extractor stays optional (AGENTS.md).

- **Layout** (`src/video_agent/layout.py`), read during the sampling pass from a 256-pixel-wide grey copy: borders are straight lines present in at least 85 % of 120 sampled frames, either across the frame away from its edge (a split) or closing a box in a corner (a camera inset); a split wins over an inset, since board edges inside a camera picture can close a box with the split line. Of the two sides the camera is the one that changes nearly every second: on the example the camera side changed in 99 % of the seconds and the screen side in 21 %, a lecture with much live coding. A side counts as camera at 50 % or more, and the screen must be under 50 % and at most half the camera's share. Without a border the frame is a screen when under 50 %, otherwise a camera (no pages). The result, with the measured shares, goes into the manifest, and `frames/<run>/layout.jpg` shows it (screen green, camera red). `--screen-region X,Y,W,H` overrides it.
- **Screen pages** (`media.screen_pages`), a second pass over the screen region at the source resolution (an inset is blacked out): one frame per second; FFmpeg's `freezedetect` compares each frame with the first frame of the current page and marks the frame at which the region has stayed within a mean difference of 0.0025 (all colour planes) for 2 s. Measured with `freezedetect` itself: a dark label on 0.8 % of a white slide changes it by 0.003-0.004; on the first 400 s of the example 0.004 gave 10 stable periods, 0.0025 14 and 0.002 17, where the quality jump at keyframes starts to count. Each page records when it appeared and how long it stayed (the `freeze_end` events). (Until 2026-10-28 `mpdecimate` did this; it is GPL-only and the installer now ships an LGPL FFmpeg, see [Bundled FFmpeg](bundled-ffmpeg.md).) The pages are then thinned on 128x72 grey copies (`media.merge_similar`), because the user found too many near repeats (2026-10-28): a page within 2.5 % of the pixels of its group's first page joins the group, which keeps its first time and last, fullest picture (bullets appearing, a finished line); the same text scrolled by less than a third of the height, as a whole or in its left or right half (an editor beside a terminal), joins too; a return to one of the last 8 pages (switching windows) is dropped; and a page shown for less than 6 s is dropped unless more than half of it is new (another window or slide), so a terminal printing in bursts or a page being typed leaves one page. On the example this went from 285 stable periods to 94 pages, with every slide kept. The 6 s are then tuned per video, as Opencast tunes its segmenter towards a segment count (user request, 2026-10-28): when the pages exceed 75 an hour (at least 30 allowed for short videos) the time goes up through 10, 15, 25 and 40 s, and below 15 an hour it goes down to 4 and 2 s; the value used is `page_shown_at_least_s` in the manifest's `limits`. Only short pages that are not mostly new respond to it, so a slide that replaces the screen is kept at any value, and the same-page threshold is never raised, since that would merge different slides. Two slides in one template can differ in about 4 %, so the same-page threshold stays below that. The pages go to `frames/<run>/screen/page-NNN-HH-MM-SS.jpg`, the manifest and a "Screen pages" section of `summary-context.md`.
- **Costs.** Two decoding passes, as before (the old periodic and scene passes are now one): on the example about 60 s each, the layout analysis about 1 s, the merging under 1 s.
- **Use of the pages.** `summarize` sends every page within its image budget (default raised from 40 to 60): one per image, or on 2x2 or 3x3 contact sheets (`media.sheets`, 1568 px wide, Anthropic's scaling width) when there are more, with the time and path of each tile in the label; the rest of the budget, at least 8, goes to whole frames. Pages are dropped, evenly and with a message, only when 3x3 sheets do not fit either. `pack --zip` holds every page, up to 40 whole frames and 2x2 sheets in `总图/`, which 提示词.md maps to times and paths for chat AIs that take few images. The skill tells Agents to look at all pages in order. The GUI lists the pages above the whole frames, shows `layout.jpg` on the 处理 tab, and lets the user drag the screen region on it and process again with it.
- **AutoSlides Extractor** (`--extract-slides`) now gets the screen region alone: for a split or inset the region is first encoded as its own video (libx264, CRF 18).
- **Limits.** Pages and representative frames stop at 500 each, spread evenly, and the run reports it; the GUI's frame field is empty (no cap) by default, and the old saved default of 36 is ignored.
- **Prior art (checked 2026-10-28).** Mature tools solve slide changes on a screen-only stream: Opencast's video segmenter (ECL-2.0) runs FFmpeg's scene score on the separately recorded presentation track, drops segments under 60 s and tunes its threshold towards about 30 segments per video; PySceneDetect (BSD-3) detects cuts and takes a crop region only by hand. Lecture-capture systems record the screen as its own track, so cutting it out of a composite is left to small projects; an independent survey of their source (henricos/extract-slides, `docs/research/slide-region-crop.md`, September 2026) found that none crops to an automatically found slide region apart from AutoSlides Extractor's contour detector, and that the best picture-in-picture detector samples pairs of frames 1 s apart across the video, as Video Agent's layout reading does. Projects without a licence (bit-admin/slide-crop and its YOLO model, patrickmineault/vid2slides) may be read for ideas but not copied.
- **Not done: cropping to the slide canvas** inside the screen region (black bars, window frames), as slide-crop and AutoSlides' auto-crop do with contours. The user judged it too rare to be worth it (2026-10-28); if needed, reimplement the contour method, do not copy unlicensed code.
- **Still pictures in a camera video** (2026-10-29). Some videos are moving stock footage most of the time, with a chart, a table or a screenshot of a document now and then (the user's example: a 5-minute talk on COVID figures). Read as a camera video, it had no pages, and of its 37 representative frames (every 60 s and at scene changes) most showed viruses, hearts or a crowd. The page search now runs on the whole frame of a camera video too: moving footage never stays still for 2 s, a chart held on screen does. On the example it gave 11 pages, 8 of them charts, tables or the disease-control page, 3 stock shots. Subtitles burnt into the picture change every 2-3 s and cut a chart shown for ten seconds into several short stays, so the 6 s rule dropped real charts: a page's stay now runs on through the following stays of the same page that start within 3 s (`page_rules.stayed`). In `summary-context.md` such pages are described as still pictures between other footage, so the last one is not taken as still on screen. A camera filming a projector still gives no pages: the speaker moves in the frame.
- **AutoSlides Extractor finds the screen pages where it is installed** (user decision, 2026-10-29). On the user's 99-minute Yanhekt screen recording it kept 115 slides to our 88, 92 of them at the same times (within 6 s). Most of its 23 more were steps of one slide and frames of a video playing on a slide, but three were slides turned within seconds (Berkeley Unix Consultant, FAQ, 最近发展趋势) that our rule for passing states dropped (shown under 6 s and not mostly new). It took 2 min 18 s. So `process` runs it on the screen region by default when it finds it (cropped for split and inset layouts), merges the steps of one slide (`page_rules.merge_steps`: within 2.5 % of the group's first slide, or all of it with more added), and its slides stand for the screen pages; our page search runs only without it or when it fails. Pages too many are fine (user: the chat AI drops them in 画面调整). `--no-extract-slides`, `AUTOSLIDES_EXTRACTOR=none` and the window's checkbox turn it off; the tests set the latter, so results do not depend on the machine.
- **A download button for it** (user decision, 2026-10-29, of two: bundling it in the installer, which its MIT licence and LGPL FFmpeg and Qt allow, would add about 90 MB and the FFmpeg 7 and Qt 6 sources to every release). 设置 → AutoSlides Extractor downloads the latest Windows portable zip from the author's GitHub releases (through the API; github.com first, then the update mirrors), checks its size and the release's SHA-256 digest, unpacks it with Windows' own tar (Git's GNU tar on PATH does not read zip files) into `<userData>/tools/AutoSlides Extractor`, and every CLI the app starts gets `AUTOSLIDES_EXTRACTOR` pointing at it unless the user set that variable. Tried on 2026-10-29: 91.9 MB from github.com, and the portable build extracts as the installed one does.
- **bit-admin's models, a future direction** (user decision, 2026-10-29). The slide classifier (MobileNetV4, MIT) and slide detector (YOLOv8, slide-crop, no licence stated) were tried on our pictures. Trained on Yanhekt screen recordings, the classifier called the COVID video's charts and the disease-control table not slides (0.79-0.93) and two stock shots slides (0.88-0.97); the detector found the browser slide in 容斥原理 (0.7) but not the projected screen in a camera recording of 线性代数 (mostly 0.06-0.15). Not used for now.
- **Not handled yet.** A layout that changes during the video (for example camera only for the first minutes) is read as a whole, so its border may not reach 85 %; then the frame counts as one region.

## Frame sampling fixes (2026-10-26 and 2026-10-27)

- Frame sampling: candidates less than 1 s apart are merged; a video without scene changes no longer reports scene detection as failed (FFmpeg 7 and 9 word an empty selection differently from older versions); reprocessing keeps the original transcript source in the manifest.
