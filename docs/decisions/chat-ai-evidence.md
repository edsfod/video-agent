# Evidence for weaker chat AIs, local subtitles, folder names — user requests (2026-10-28, for 0.9.0)

After DeepSeek's web chat summarised the fourth NJU lecture under the revised rules and still retold it, with time ranges out of order and pictures from other points, and after the user listed what else went wrong:

- **The transcript marks the picture on screen.** summary-context.md groups the transcript lines under a `▶ screen page from HH:MM:SS: <path>` line (or the frame, for a video without screen pages), as yanhe-agent's and yanhekit's `query` start each run with the slide then on screen; the model links what it sees there instead of matching times itself. A context written before is rewritten on the next pack or summary, without processing again.
- **The points missed, once more at the end.** Chat material ends with a short reminder of the rules a chat AI lost over 120,000 characters (notes not retelling, sections in order, the picture of the point, 补充：, the speaker's strength, the code block); the rules themselves stay in the template. Claude judgment on the wording.
- **Pages built up step by step are one page.** Page thinning also merges a page into the next when the next keeps what it showed and adds to it (`media._added`: fewer than 3 % of the first page's content pixels changed, or the first page nearly blank); the group keeps its first time and its last, complete picture. Steps of one slide and a chat growing in a demo had been kept as pages of their own. yanhe-agent and yanhekit leave this to an Agent looking at contact sheets, which a chat AI cannot do.
- **Frames a chat AI asks for.** The material with pictures tells a chat AI to link a missing picture as `补截:HH:MM:SS`; the import takes that frame into frames/extra and points the link at it (also inside a link Gemini wrapped in a search URL). A time outside the video becomes `补截失败:…`.
- **Local subtitles without a click.** Videos added from disk are processed at once when 下载或添加后自动处理 is on, so a same-name subtitle file is read in (`show` reports `subtitles`, where processing would take them from). A local video's audio is not uploaded by default (user decision, 2026-10-28): for videos without subtitles the window asks first, with 用必剪转录 (saying the audio goes to Bilibili), 只处理画面 or 先不处理. `process --subtitles FILE` (GUI: 字幕 → 导入字幕…) takes an SRT or WebVTT file of any name as the transcript.
- **Slides made already** (user decision, 2026-10-28, option (b) of three: a named convention of one tool plus a manual route, rather than a rule that only looks general). `process` takes AutoSlides' own folder for a screen recording it downloaded, by AutoSlides' naming (`screen_<name>.mp4` beside `slides_<name>/`, `slide_extractor.autoslides_folder`), and `--slides FOLDER` (GUI: 画面 → 导入课件…) any folder of slide images. Images are copied into the video's folder; AutoSlides' `timeline.json` gives their times (the format its Extractor writes, read by the same code), and images without one take the time of the screen page they look like. Slides with times stand for the screen pages in the transcript marks and in the pictures sent to models and chat AIs, since AutoSlides filters and crops them.
- **Folder names keep Chinese.** A video's processing folder is named from its title with letters of any script kept (it dropped every non-ASCII letter, leaving `screen_-_-8-_-_-3-__c73687s923162`); a folder under the old name is renamed when first used and the collection files that link into it follow.
- **Burned-in subtitles** (in the picture, as on the user's 新冠 video): only OCR could read them, which the user ruled out; BCut transcription, now on by default, covers such videos. Left open.

## Pictures as material, over several rounds — user requests (2026-10-29)

On the 新冠 video (stock footage with a chart now and then) DeepSeek's web chat linked the frame grouped with each passage, stock shots included, because the context said to link the picture on screen while the lines were said; it could neither drop the useless frames nor get the missing ones, and the `补截:HH:MM:SS` frames went into the summary unseen. The user asked for a chat AI to reach, over several rounds of copying, what an Agent does, and for the pictures to be used where they say more than the transcript rather than matched by time.

- **Pictures are material.** The rules (`prompts/summary.md`) and the chat reminder say to read figures, documents, formulas and code out of the pictures, above all what the speaker does not say, and to link a picture only where it shows the point; stock footage, a background or the speaker is not linked. The context's grouping line says the same.
- **A 画面调整 section.** Material with pictures asks a chat AI to end its summary with `不用：<path>` for pictures it does not need and `补截：<start>–<end> <what>` where one is missing. The import records the first in `picture-review.json`, and they leave every later pack, context and model request; for each span it takes up to four candidates that differ from each other, from runs of seconds showing the same picture, longest first and only pictures held for 2 s or more when the span has any (choosing only by difference gave three shots of a moving corridor before the chart). On the example a request for 1:08–1:22 gave the one chart that page search had dropped. `pack --follow-up` gives the next round: the candidates and the summary as saved; an answer without the section ends the rounds. **Claude judgment:** the section's form, four candidates, 10 s after a single time.
- **Keyword frames removed.** The pointed-at frames (a list of pointing words in the transcript) took 「在这个图上」 in a lecture on graphs for pointing and were taken as the words began, before the thing was shown; the user judged the approach wrong. The chat AI, which reads the whole transcript, now asks for the pictures it misses. Old manifests keep their `pointed_frames`, and the frames stay in `frames/extra/`.
- **A DeepSeek route** (user decision, same day): DeepSeek's web chat in a window of the app, driven as a person would use it (the official page at chat.deepseek.com, signed in there by the user, kept in its own session), so the rounds run without copying; not a reverse-engineered API, whose projects warn of account bans. The summary page's 「DeepSeek 网页」 route packs a folder (as 导出文件夹, up to the same file limit), attaches the pictures, types the prompt, sends it with DeepThink and Search on (user decision: Search for the 补充 a summary may add; the page in English, user decision), waits until the send button stops showing the stop square, reads the answer's code block, imports it, and runs `pack --follow-up` rounds while the answer asks for pictures (at most 6, said when reached). A first summary is current and each round replaces it; beside an existing summary every round is a version, with 第 N 轮 as its note. One DeepSeek task ran at a time (one window) until 2026-10-30; see below. Tested on 2026-10-29 with the user's account: DeepSeek read a chart's shape (where the highest bar is, how many peaks), so it sees pictures, not only their text; a first round with 48 pictures and the full prompt took 1 s to attach and 42-165 s to answer. Whether it reads contact sheets at its image size is still to be tested.
- **Link text names the picture.** On that test DeepSeek still linked stock shots and wrote no 画面调整 section. The rules now ask for link text that says what the picture shows (`[<kind>：<what it shows>](frames/…)`) and for a check of the pictures before finishing; on the next run it linked no stock shot and read figures out of the charts that were not said. It still asked for no picture where the transcript does not mention one, which it cannot know. The examples in the prompts are placeholders, not content from a video (user decision: a concrete example labels pictures for the model).
- **A number printed on every picture** (2026-10-29). With 48 numbered files attached, DeepSeek twice described a stock shot as another picture's chart: it matched pictures to the table by the order they were uploaded. Every picture packed for a chat AI, each page on a contact sheet and each candidate of a next round now carries `#<number> <time>` in its lower left corner, and the table lists the number beside the path; the prompt says to go by the printed number. The bundled FFmpeg has no drawtext (no font library), so the label is drawn from a 5x7 bitmap font of digits and a few signs and laid over with `overlay`. On the next run one picture was still off by one, out of nine linked.
- **Guessing where a picture is missing.** The user asked for the chat AI, not a word list, to say where pictures are needed: the prompt asks it to guess from the transcript (a list of figures read out, a chart or curve described, a document read clause by clause, 「看这里」) where something was on screen that no attached picture shows, and to ask for that span; asking too often costs little.
- **DeepSeek busy** (user report, 2026-10-29). An upload that fails or does not finish in 5 minutes, or an answer that is only a busy notice, is tried again up to three times: the conversation's page is loaded again (the conversation stays, the box empties), the files attached and the text typed anew; after a busy notice it waits 30 s first.
- **Failed uploads one by one** (user report, 2026-10-30: with 49 pictures attached, every retry uploaded all of them again, and three times in a row one failed). A picture whose upload failed is removed from the message and attached again alone, up to three times; after that the message goes without it, and the task output says which (Claude judgment: one picture missing is better than no summary). Uploads that do not finish, and busy answers, are still tried again whole. Checked on the real page with one upload blocked: only that file was sent again.
- **The DeepSeek window keeps drawing** (user report, 2026-10-30: two answers were read back incomplete and their import failed, while the copy button on the page gave the whole answer). Read by script, not from a screenshot: the answer was whole in one code block, but while a window is covered by others, minimised or off the screen, Windows' occlusion tracking makes Chromium count it as hidden, and the page drew no frames (0 animation frames a second), so the streamed answer never reached the page's elements. The app now starts with Chromium's `CalculateNativeWinOcclusion` feature off and the DeepSeek window unthrottled; it then drew covered, minimised and off the screen (about 110 frames a second, the code block whole). Both are needed: unthrottled alone still drew nothing. When an import fails, the answer goes into the task's log.
- **DeepSeek summaries side by side** (user request, 2026-10-30). Each DeepSeek task has a window of its own, all signed in through the same session; a window whose task ended serves the next task, and a window still on the sign-in page loads again once another window is signed in. They count as summaries in the queue (设置 → 总结, 2 by default) instead of a pool of one of their own (Claude judgment: no other limit to set). Two conversations at once were not tried on the real page; DeepSeek has a "Too many concurrent requests" message, which is now treated as a busy notice.
- **An answer that never starts** (user report, 2026-10-30: 正在分析图片 for 8 minutes, until the user sent the message again by hand). Read by script: the page's code shows 正在分析图片 while the answer has no thinking and no text yet and the attached pictures are not read, and the server had no message from that send. A send whose answer has not started in 3 minutes is now tried again like a busy notice (the page loaded again, the files attached and the text typed anew); the 49 pictures of the same message were read and the thinking begun within seconds.
- **Finding the new answer** (user report, same day: the third round's answer was written and never taken). The page does not keep every earlier answer: by the third round the first round's blocks were gone, so the count of answer blocks did not grow and the app waited on. The blocks on the page are now marked before sending, and the answer is the last block without the mark.
- **Rounds of 复查 until nothing is added** (user report, 2026-10-30: DeepSeek's answers often left out the 画面调整 section, so the rounds ended after one, at most two, errors and all, and some were thin; read from nine of the user's chats by script). Of those answers, one wrote no 画面调整 section, the second rounds never asked for more (their prompt said to leave the section out when there was nothing to change), one put mishearings under 需确认 instead of 字幕改正, and the shortest (6,569 characters for 100 minutes, after 4,867 characters of thinking) had 17 propositions. The checks run on them found 20 minutes of speech no part of 详细内容 covered in one, 9 in another. Now (user decision): every answer imported from a chat AI is checked for the template's sections, the 画面调整 section (written as 无 when there is nothing to change), the spans of speech no part of 详细内容 covers and times past the end, and what is found is sent back by name in the next round; a first draft always goes to one round of 复查, in which the chat AI goes through the transcript again and ends with a 复查 section of what it added and corrected, and sections and spans it leaves out on purpose, with why. The rounds go on while a 复查 adds or corrects something, a check finds something or pictures are wanted, at most 6. Whether a summary is detailed enough is judged by whether every point, reason, example, number, definition, condition and conclusion said is in it, not by its length or minutes per part (user decision: those are not what makes a summary detailed); the reminder at the end of the prompt no longer says to write notes that can be scanned quickly. Agent drafts (`--import` alone) have no rounds.
- **Contact sheets are read** (tested 2026-10-29). The 新冠 video packed into 12 files (its pages on 2x2 contact sheets) came back with every picture link right and figures read off the sheets (21.7 %, 15.8 %, 80 people, 3.57 %), no worse than 48 single pictures; one table was described as a line chart. 3x3 sheets are not tested yet. In that answer a picture was linked and also listed as 不用; a picture the summary links now stays in use.
- **Rounds on a two-hour lecture** (tested 2026-10-29, 容斥原理, 132 slides from AutoSlides Extractor on 3x3 contact sheets, 49 files). DeepSeek guessed from the transcript that a program's output at 1:20-1:23 was shown more fully than the attached pictures, asked for that span, was sent four candidates in the second round, linked two of them (the outputs for n=3 and n=4, as checked) and asked for nothing more. The pictures checked on the sheets were described right.
